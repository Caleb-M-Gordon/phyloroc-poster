YOUR FIELD GUIDE

A limb can tell a story.

Gordon and colleagues compared limb proportions across amniotes to predict aquatic habits and soft-tissue flippers. This explorer connects the paper’s conclusions to the underlying specimen-level data.

How to explore

Choose a tip on the tree, search a name, or filter a lineage. Swap specimens and tree topologies to see which results change. Try the prediction challenge to test your intuition.

Reading the results

Model probability is the mean predicted probability in Table S07, not an observation or a guarantee. “Predicted: yes” uses the table’s final verdict, based on its statistical test against the model’s optimal threshold—not a 50% cutoff. “Not supported” means that test did not classify the specimen as having the phenotype; it does not prove absence.

Fixed score means the phenotype was assigned a priori in the analysis. Its 0/1 value is not a model-derived confidence estimate. Unknown model entries stay unavailable.

Highly / fully aquatic means spending the vast majority of time in water, or being effectively confined to it. A negative result can still describe a moderately aquatic animal. The two categories are combined, not distinguished by this classifier. Flipper results refer to the selected limb pair.

What is shown

The tree is pruned from the eight original input Newick trees to extinct Pan-Reptilia in Table S01. Specimen replicates are collapsed to the first encountered tip for each taxon. Branches show topology, not elapsed time. Dates are dataset fossil occurrence bounds, not exact biological lifespan estimates. Genus-level “sp” entries retain their original scope.

The default is supertree1, the all-data raw model, forelimb acropodium-to-zeugopodium ratio (f_AZR), and bin_eco1_v3 for aquatic habits. Hindlimb selection changes only the flipper result to h_AZR / bin_hflip. Aquatic results remain forelimb-based. Specimens are never averaged together. No models are rerun here.

Each taxon has its own stylized illustration, with individual proportions, markings and silhouette details. These are illustrative designs, not anatomical reconstructions. Cyan paddles share one color and shape across all taxa and indicate a positive flipper result for that limb pair; forelimbs and hindlimbs are evaluated separately. If a limb-model entry is missing, an explicit Flippered score in Table S01 is used; otherwise dashed limbs indicate unavailable results. Aquatic Yes results swim; No results stand and sway on a shoreline; unavailable results use a gray background. Shoreline placement does not exclude semi-aquatic habits. Challenge mode conceals the visual phenotype cues until reveal.
